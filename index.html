Everything your clinic does, finally in one place.

Most clinics juggle 4 different tools and 3 WhatsApp groups. clinicKU gives every role exactly one page to do their job — and everyone sees the same truth.

Front Desk That Moves Fast

Register a patient in seconds. Today's queue shows status, room and contact at a glance — with one-tap Call and WhatsApp (Malaysian numbers auto-formatted to +60) to call patients back without dialing mistakes.

Doctor Does It All On One Page

Diagnosis, remarks, prescriptions pulled straight from live inventory, and a printable medical certificate with certificate number and rest-period dates — generated in one click.

Pharmacy With Proof

Every dispensed item gets a QR code. Scan to verify — wrong item, unverified stock, or double-scanning gets caught instantly. Stock deducts itself, and low-stock warnings appear before you run out.

Cashier Closes The Loop

See the next patient to pay with a suggested amount already calculated. Generate quotation, invoice or receipt, take payment, and the patient's status flips to a blue "✓ Paid" badge automatically.

The Journey Tracker

Registered → Diagnosis → Prescribed → Dispensed → Paid. Every Doctor, Pharmacy and Cashier page shows exactly which stage each patient is at — and what still needs to be done. No more "sapa pegang fail ni?"

Inventory You Control

Add, edit and delete medication stock with auto-generated item codes, reorder levels and In stock / Low stock / Out of stock alerts — prescription dropdowns read straight from it.
